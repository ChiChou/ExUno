# On-device language coach

The optional coach in Grammar is available for Italian, Spanish, French and Portuguese on iOS 26 or later when the Apple on-device system model is available. Latin is not offered. Role play starts from a bundled catalog of everyday scenes, with two prepared exchanges per scene and suggested replies, and retrieves matching words from the learner’s selected levels. Quizzes use a selected lesson restricted to those levels. Its rule and up to three course examples are supplied as context, and a random lesson example anchors the quiz answer. These materials are the existing prebuilt grammar packs; their online sources and licenses are documented in ATTRIBUTIONS.md and docs/LICENSING.md. The prepared path does not need an available model; free-form replies and generated quizzes do. Nothing is fetched at runtime for this feature.

The app checks quiz structure and that the answer matches the source example. This does not establish that a generated sentence, distractor, explanation or correction is linguistically correct. Review output with fluent speakers before relying on it for instruction. The current conversation and unsent draft are saved locally per study language, including the prepared scene’s progress. Reopening the coach restores it; a new role play replaces that language’s saved conversation. After a model reply is interrupted, the learner can retry, and a new model session receives the saved dialogue as context. Conversations remain limited in length.

For a model test, enable Apple Intelligence and wait for the model download on a supported Mac and run a matching macOS, Xcode and iOS simulator runtime. Alternatively test on a supported iPhone. On macOS 27, `fm respond` can exercise the on-device model separately, but it does not test the app or its simulator integration. Test airplane mode after setup, unavailable-model and iOS 18–25 fallbacks, all four supported languages, and Latin navigation.
